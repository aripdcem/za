ZA is an ad-free game platform: 26 games in one app. None of them shows ads, asks for permissions or collects data. There is not even an INTERNET permission; everything works offline.

OUR PROMISE
• 0 ads: no ad SDK
• 0 trackers: no analytics or tracking library
• 0 permissions: the app requests none
• Pure play: scores and settings stay on your device

GAMES
Arcade: Blok (falling blocks), Snake, Kuyu (descend the well, daily well), Geçit (road crossing, daily mode), Balkon (aim from the balcony: pumpkin seeds, water balloons or spit), Viraj (pseudo-3D racing, daily track), Filo (vertical space shooter, daily fleet), Raket (paddle game: vs the computer, a friend or the wall; daily wall), Tuşe (piano tiles: seven public-domain melodies; daily piece), Uçurtma (hold to climb; rooftops, wires and rival kites; missions and gear; daily sky), Dalgıç (rescue divers by submarine; oxygen, sharks, mines, currents; daily sea), Bostan (lane defense: wells, sprinklers, scarecrows, hives, traps; every level generated winnable; three difficulties, daily garden), Sincap (climb a plane tree: tap left/right to jump to the next branch; dry branches, snakes, crows and a cat gaining on you; daily tree), Çekirge (formation shooter: drag to walk, tap to spray, one-shot rule; hay bales, the queen; daily field)
Puzzle: 2048, Sudoku (three levels, notes, unique solution), Minesweeper, Kakuro (three sizes, unique solution), Reyon (planogram puzzle, compliance audit and sales layout; daily shelf)
Word (Turkish): Beş Harf (daily five-letter word), Kıskaç (alphabetical squeeze), Türetme (words from letters), Dizgi (pass-and-play word board, 2-4 players)
Board and numbers: Tavla (backgammon: Classic, Tapa, Hapis; vs computer or a friend), Vergici (Taxman), Toplam Kapma (Number Scrabble)

FEATURES
• Daily puzzles: everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day, generated from the date with no server
• Share your result: the Share button renders a result card and a short text
• Turkish dictionaries built in; Turkish keyboard and alphabetical order
• Sound and haptics toggle with one tap; left-hand mode
• Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/aripdcem/za
